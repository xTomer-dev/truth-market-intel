Block 2 satellite launch occurred on schedule in August. We now have 17 satellites in orbit across our early constellation. AT&T has signed a definitive commercial agreement and Verizon remains in final contract negotiations. Cash position improved following our $200 million convertible note offering, extending runway to 22 months. Burn rate increased to $34 million per quarter reflecting accelerated constellation build. Peak throughput testing achieved 64 Mbps on latest generation hardware. Spectrum interference risk in the 850 MHz band remains a regulatory concern pending FCC ruling expected Q4 2024. We are cautious about guidance given regulatory uncertainty, but remain on track for commercial launch to general consumers by mid-2025.
