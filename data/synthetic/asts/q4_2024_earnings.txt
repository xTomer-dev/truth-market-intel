We ended 2024 with 24 Block 2 satellites in orbit. Commercial service with AT&T launched January 15, 2025, ahead of the mid-2025 target. Verizon commercial agreement signed December 2024. Vodafone signed an MOU for European markets. Cash position $312 million, burn rate $38 million per quarter, runway approximately 20 months. We raised guidance for 2025: targeting 45 satellites by year-end and revenue of $40-60 million. Management credibility improved significantly with on-time delivery of the Block 2 constellation. Competitive pressure from Starlink direct-to-cell remains elevated but we believe our carrier-embedded model provides a distinct moat.
